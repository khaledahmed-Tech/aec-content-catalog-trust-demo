# Interview Positioning

## Interview-safe positioning statement

“I built this as a synthetic product case study, not as an attempt to reproduce a commercial content catalog or present myself as a BIM authoring expert. My adjacent platform experience is in governed publishing, discovery, access, reusable assets, lifecycle management, telemetry, and adoption. The bridge is the product problem, not a claim that APIs, developer templates, and BIM content are technically equivalent.”

## How to introduce the prototype

Lead with the product problem: practitioners need to find appropriate approved content quickly, and administrators need to understand demand, lifecycle state, and bottlenecks without relying on opaque scores or tribal knowledge.

Then state the boundary: the prototype is synthetic, browser-local, and deliberately avoids real design files, technical validation, production integrations, customer data, and vendor affiliation.

## Why it was built

The prototype was built to demonstrate how a product manager or platform product leader can translate governed reuse into a visible loop:

1. Discovery through search and filters.
2. Trust evaluation through explainable evidence.
3. Demand capture when content is missing or needs revision.
4. Admin triage with deterministic priority and audit notes.
5. Telemetry that turns workflow friction into product investigation.

## Product thinking, not production software

This is a product-framing artifact. It is useful for discussing decisions, assumptions, workflow seams, telemetry definitions, and validation plans. It is not evidence that the underlying thresholds are correct for every firm, discipline, or content type.

## Likely interviewer questions

### Are you a BIM practitioner?

No. I would not present this as BIM practitioner expertise. I used synthetic metadata to frame the product problem and would validate the terminology, thresholds, and workflows with BIM managers, content admins, designers, and engineers.

### Does this integrate with Revit or AutoCAD?

No. Revit and AutoCAD appear only as plain synthetic metadata labels. The app does not call design-tool APIs, parse files, validate compatibility, or insert content into any authoring environment.

### Is the trust score a quality or certification score?

No. It is a deterministic catalog-confidence indicator for governance evidence and metadata. It is not a certification of design quality, engineering fitness, safety, constructability, code compliance, standards compliance, file integrity, or production readiness.

### Is this an Autodesk competitor?

No. It is an independent synthetic product case study. It does not use Autodesk logos, screenshots, trade dress, APIs, product claims, or affiliated branding, and it is not a feature-for-feature reproduction of any commercial catalog.

## Product decisions and trade-offs

- **Trust is explainable:** users see component evidence rather than only a number.
- **Governance outranks popularity:** high usage cannot make an ineligible record selectable.
- **Failed search is a workflow input:** zero results become structured demand, not a dead end.
- **Admin priority is transparent:** point contributions are visible and cannot be manually overwritten.
- **Telemetry closes the loop:** metrics connect discovery, reuse, blocked attempts, requests, and catalog health.
- **Scope stays safe:** no files, APIs, authentication, AI-generated design content, production analytics, or vendor affiliation.

## Discovery questions to ask Autodesk or a hiring manager

- Which signals do designers actually use to decide whether content is trustworthy and appropriate?
- How do firms define Approved, Superseded, Deprecated, and Needs Revision across disciplines?
- Which metadata fields and parameter conventions matter most for search and reuse by content type?
- When does content freshness require a formal review date rather than a last-updated proxy?
- Who owns request triage, content creation, discipline approval, and final publication?
- What is the right balance between strict governance and the risk that users return to personal libraries?
- Which telemetry signals indicate true reuse and value when a catalog is integrated into an authoring workflow?
